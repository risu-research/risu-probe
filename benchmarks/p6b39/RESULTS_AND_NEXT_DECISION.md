## Primary normal false-positive denominator and the crucial measurement limit

Preregistered normal organizations: **four (N1–N4)**. Every one of the four has some executed original authored code or original author test evidence, but execution strengths differ. **Zero have a verified independently authored *original external consumer* running end-to-end with an independently witnessed downstream outcome under the preregistered status/channel contract.** Hence:

- Independent original external consumer FPR eligible `m = 0`.
- Confirmed false alarms in such a cohort: **unmeasured**, not `0/4`, not `0/1`.
- Population FPR, recall, precision, overall accuracy: **UNMEASURED**. Do not calculate Wilson interval on `m = 0`.
- A separate, narrower original-server statement is justified: Geolonia original producer with controlled caller: **0 discrepancies / 1 independently authored producer organization** under its declared status and JSON-mirror contract. Seven earlier tests and three later tests are repeated/related within the **same** organization, not ten independent organizations. A Wilson 95% *producer-cohort discrepancy* upper bound for 0/1 is approximately **0.793**, far too imprecise for an ecosystem claim.
- KiraAI intentionally truncating three outputs and Appwrite deliberately redacting fields are explicitly permitted by product policies. Deliberately scoring them under `text:"exact"` or `structured:"exact"` instead of their documented policy is **not detector false-positive evidence**.
- Source-only original tests and controlled MCP peers cannot substitute for original external client execution. Preserve every downgrade in the denominator and result files.

## What is now actually established

1. A source-neutral rule frozen before the B39 selection accepted native status and structured content under the documented original server normal policy in a separate new organization. This reinforces B38 two-positive-organization evidence but does not prove low external-client FPR.
2. Original third-party repair behavior is demonstrable across distinct producer and consumer mechanisms. FastMCP and MCPProxy-Go both have official upstream RED-before/GREEN-after tests; Otari has exact original formatter body and live stdio resource results. These results support the *semantic loss problem's existence*, not new unsupervised discovery or generic detector recall.
3. Four developer-intended normal policies reveal the indispensable product requirement: distinguish full preservation, intentional redaction, bounded output and compact views before emitting loss alerts. A checker that assumes raw equality for every channel is unsuitable for real adopters.
4. Data, original-source SHA pins, selected policies, proof classes and workflow results remain isolated from `main`. Nothing was published to PyPI. B39 cases became exposed when executed and cannot be reused as unseen B40 samples.

## Decision and B40 prerequisite

**Keep the public product pinned and unchanged.** Do not merge the B38 generic checker based on B39 alone: trustworthy observed consumer witnesses and actual external-client normal negative controls remain the bottleneck.

For B40, first freeze a *new disjoint* cohort of independently authored, runnable MCP **clients** with existing owner-documented intentional output policies plus known repaired error/structured regressions. Require a real original client/adapter call, same native controlled server on before/after where applicable, raw JSON-RPC evidence, actual downstream consumer outcome, source Git blob check, explicitly preregistered per-channel output contract, failure and normal controls, and an independent consumer-level FPR denominator. If original tool consumers cannot be executed unchanged, count them as `NOT_EXECUTED`, not as clean negatives. No B39-informed rule tuning should be evaluated on B39 as if it were unseen.
